Investor Hype Score (IHS)
Definition

A quantitative estimate of how strongly a stock’s investor interest is driven by narrative, attention, and speculative enthusiasm rather than underlying financial fundamentals.

Total Score = 0–100
Sum of five dimensions, each scored 0–20.

1. Retail Attention Intensity (0–20)
Measures the level of retail investor discussion and visibility.
Signals
Reddit mentions (WSB, r/stocks, etc.)
X/Twitter cashtag frequency
Google Trends spikes
YouTube finance coverage
Discord / StockTwits activity
Scoring
0–4: Rarely discussed
5–9: Moderate chatter
10–14: Frequently discussed
15–17: Dominant retail topic
18–20: Meme-level omnipresence

2. Narrative Strength & Virality (0–20)
Evaluates how compelling and shareable the investment story is.
Common narratives
AI leader
Next Tesla
Industry disruptor
Cult CEO
Short squeeze
Turnaround
Category creator
National champion
Scoring
0–4: No clear story
5–9: Niche thesis
10–14: Clear theme
15–17: Viral narrative
18–20: Cultural phenomenon

3. Price Behavior Consistent with Hype (0–20)
Identifies speculative price dynamics.
Indicators
Parabolic rallies
Gap-up moves
Momentum bursts
Retail-driven runs
High volatility vs peers
Weak linkage to earnings revisions
Scoring
0–4: Stable valuation trading
5–9: Moderate momentum
10–14: Hype-like runs
15–17: Repeated speculative spikes
18–20: Meme-stock dynamics

4. Valuation vs Fundamentals Gap (0–20)
Measures how much expectations exceed current business reality.
Indicators
Revenue multiple vs sector
Profitability absence with premium valuation
Long-dated TAM narrative
Extreme forward growth assumptions
Negative FCF with premium pricing
Scoring
0–4: Fundamentals aligned
5–9: Mild premium
10–14: Optimism priced in
15–17: Extreme expectations
18–20: Story exceeds business

5. Retail-Speculation Structure (0–20)
Structural mechanics that amplify hype behavior.
Indicators
High short interest
Options volume vs shares
Call skew
Gamma squeeze potential
Influencer promotion
Retail ownership share
Fractional trading popularity
Scoring
0–4: Institution-driven
5–9: Balanced ownership
10–14: Retail-heavy
15–17: Speculation-prone
18–20: Squeeze-capable
Final Score Interpretation
0–20: No hype
21–40: Low hype
41–60: Moderate hype
61–80: High hype
81–100: Extreme / meme-level

Strict Scoring Procedure
Use a 30-day observation window
Score each of the five dimensions
Sum scores
Cap at 100
Round to nearest integer

Calibration Anchors
Low hype (10–30)
JPM, PG, KO

Moderate hype (40–60)
AMD, SHOP, DIS

High hype (60–80)
NVDA (AI era), TSLA typical years, PLTR

Extreme hype (80–100)
GME 2021, AMC 2021, DWAC 2022
